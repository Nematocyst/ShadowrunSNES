Rewrite any line in the game

Every piece of text in Shadowrun is stored compressed: 1,589 texts covering conversations, messages, keywords and names. This editor decodes them from your ROM, lets you rewrite them with a live preview in the game's own font, and writes an IPS patch. Longer texts are moved to free space automatically.

  1. Open your Shadowrun (USA) ROM. Vanilla, Runner Pack and Expanded Dialogue ROMs all work. It stays in your browser.
  2. Find a line by searching for its words, the person who says it, or a keyword.
  3. Export: you get a .zip with the .ips patch for that ROM.

Put | before a word to emphasise it, as the game does for keywords. Line breaks are automatic.

Special characters

The game's font has a few extra symbols, plus blanks of 1 to 8 pixels (␣) that menus use to line things up. Click one to insert it; in the text it shows as a code like {08}. Characters the font lacks (# % & + ; < > @ [ ] ^ ` ~ and accents) can't be used.
