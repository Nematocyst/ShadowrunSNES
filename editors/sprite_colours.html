Recolour and repaint any character

Most characters in Shadowrun share their colours: Jake's palette is also used by 117 other sprites. This tool gives a character a palette of its own, so you can recolour Jake without recolouring half of Seattle. You can still change a shared palette for everyone when that is what you want.

  1. Open your Shadowrun (USA) ROM. Vanilla and Runner Pack ROMs both work. It stays in your browser.
  2. Pick a character, click a colour swatch and choose a new colour. The preview animates in the new colours.
  3. Or turn on Paint pixels and repaint the character's pixel art with its palette's colours.
  4. Export: you get a .zip with the .ips patch for that ROM. Apply it after the Runner Pack.

Repaint the character's pixel art with the colours of its palette: pencil, fill, colour picker (or right-click), copy and paste between frames (also mirrored, for the opposite facing), and a PNG round trip for drawing in another program.

Palette budget

The SNES shows 8 sprite palettes at once. The game uses up to 7 in busy scenes, so one own palette always fits; if a scene ever runs out, a character with its own palette falls back to its old shared colours instead of breaking the picture.
